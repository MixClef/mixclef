AboutAt home in any genre

I am a New York cellist who records and performs across a wide range of styles, including hip hop, rock, pop, classical, folk, and new age music.

I have recorded with Immortal Technique, Christine Lavin, and Michal, and I bring the same care to a string arrangement on a hip hop track as to a classical program or a folk session.

ServicesWhat I offer

Studio

Cello parts for records, film, television, and other media.

  • Session work in New York studios
  • Section overdubs and layered parts
  • Arranging and writing cello parts

Live

I perform live on cello with artists and bands who hire me for their shows.
